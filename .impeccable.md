## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, job seekers, Shagun's friends, and first-time visitors who want a concise, trustworthy view of Shagun Nagpal's business-intelligence profile, career, projects, skills, and experience.

### Brand Personality
Warm, credible, and helpful. The experience should feel personal and welcoming while remaining polished enough for professional hiring conversations.

### Aesthetic Direction
Extend the portfolio's existing warm editorial visual language. Shagun AI should use Shagun's provided avatar, feel like a thoughtful portfolio companion, and avoid generic customer-support or futuristic chatbot styling.

### Design Principles
- Make professional information easy to discover through natural conversation.
- Prioritize concise, evidence-based answers grounded in Shagun's portfolio.
- Welcome a broad audience without losing credibility with recruiters and hiring managers.
- Protect private information and keep conversations relevant to Shagun's career and role fit.
- Use progressive disclosure, accessible interactions, and clear system feedback.
